Privacy policy

Last updated September 29, 2026.

gmail-connectors is a personal tool run by its owner on the owner's own computer. It connects only to Gmail accounts that the owner signs into and approves.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, the tool reads, searches, labels, and drafts messages in the connected Gmail accounts through the Gmail API.

Where data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Email content is read on demand and is not copied to any server, database, or third party. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page, and the locally stored tokens can be deleted at any time.

Contact

Questions go to the owner at the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
